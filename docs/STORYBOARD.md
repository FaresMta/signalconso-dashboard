# Storyboard

**Audience:** any consumer, and public bodies who want to know where to act.
**Core question:** what do consumers report, when and where, and what happens next?

| # | Step | Tab | Message | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | Context | Millions of consumer reports exist; what do they tell us? | KPIs, definitions |
| 2 | Context | What | A few sectors concentrate most reports; volume changed over time | Trend, top-N bars, before/after |
| 3 | Insight | When | Reports follow a yearly rhythm that depends on the sector | Seasonality heatmap |
| 4 | Insight | Where | Per head, some départements stand out | Map, ranking, histogram |
| 5 | Insight | Then what? | Many reports never get a reply | Funnel, reply rate by sector |
| 6 | Extra | Then what? | How much would better follow-up change? | What-if simulator |
| 7 | Extra | Your check | What does it mean for my area and sector? | Personal comparison |
| 8 | Trust | Data & limits | What can and cannot be concluded | Validation table, limits |
| 9 | Implications | Takeaways | What to do next | Computed findings, recommendations |
